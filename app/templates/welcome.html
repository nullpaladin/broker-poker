Request your data back from data brokers

Broker Poker fills in the privacy-request forms of hundreds of data brokers for you, so you can ask them to send you a copy of your data, stop selling it, and (optionally) delete it. It also helps you email the brokers that only accept email.

How it works

  1. Enter the details brokers need to find you (name, address, email, phone…).
  2. Pick which rights to use and how to handle CAPTCHAs.
  3. Review the list of sites, then start. Each site opens in a browser, the form is filled in, and the result is shown live.

Your state's law decides which rights apply. If your state has no privacy law, requests may not be honored.

Practice runs are the default. A practice run fills every form and takes screenshots but never submits. Sending real requests needs an explicit confirmation.

Legal disclaimer

This tool is for exercising your own privacy rights over your own information. Don't use it for anyone else's data or in any way that breaks the law. You are acting on your own behalf — this app does not act as an "authorized agent". It is provided as-is, with no guarantee that any broker will honor a request.

Privacy practices

  • Everything runs on your computer. Your details are stored encrypted on this machine only.
  • The maintainer receives no data, except what you choose to put in a GitHub issue or pull request.
  • The only network traffic is to the broker sites themselves. Your information goes only to the brokers you run.
  • No AI service ever sees your information.
